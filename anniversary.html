Anniversary

synopsis

Faustine has been planning tonight for months. Her dress is picked out, the dinner is booked in her head, and every detail of the anniversary she shares with Aveon is in place. Then a chance encounter in her building's elevator, and a car that won't pull out of the parking lot, start to unravel the evening. As the night strays further from the script she wrote, Faustine's grip on what is real begins to slip.

Anniversary is a 12-minute horror film about obsession, the stories we tell ourselves about love, and how far someone will go to protect a fantasy.
