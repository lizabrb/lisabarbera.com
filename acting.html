about

Lisa is a versatile actor drawn to coming-of-age stories and nuanced, character-driven work.

After moving to Vancouver from France in 2025, she trained full-time for a year, working with instructors including Peter New, Tasya Teles, Cassidy Bot-Devire, and David C. Jones. She continues to train and is always looking for new ways to challenge herself and grow as an artist.

Currently unrepresented and available for auditions and bookings — contact me.
